Hospital Name: City Care Hospital
Location: Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh
Hospital Type: Multi-specialty hospital

City Care Hospital provides outpatient consultations across multiple medical departments.

Patients can visit the hospital for consultations, general health checkups, and specialist appointments.

Appointments can be booked through the hospital appointment system or at the reception.

This is a demonstration project. All hospital information, doctors, timings, and fees are sample data and should not be treated as real hospital information.
